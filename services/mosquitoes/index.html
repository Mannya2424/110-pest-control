Mosquito Control in Broward & Miami-Dade

Mosquitoes breed in even small amounts of standing water, and in South Florida that means most of the year. We treat the shady, humid spots where they rest and help you get rid of breeding sites so you can enjoy your yard again.

Signs you need us

  • Bites every time you step outside, especially at dawn and dusk
  • Mosquitoes resting in shrubs and shady corners
  • Standing water in planters, gutters or toys
  • Larvae ("wigglers") in birdbaths or buckets

How to help prevent them

  • Dump standing water at least once a week: plant saucers, buckets, toys and tarps
  • Change birdbath and pet water often
  • Keep gutters clear so water drains
  • Repair torn window and door screens
